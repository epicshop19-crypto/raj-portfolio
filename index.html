Who I am

Crafting worlds
one frame at a time.

I'm Rajkumar M. — a freelance illustrator and concept artist based in India. I'm passionate about creating thoughtful, illustration-driven visual experiences that connect with people. I'm detail-oriented, hardworking, and always eager to learn and evolve creatively.

With a B.Sc in Visual Communication from Nehru Arts & Science College, Coimbatore, and 7+ years of freelance experience since 2018, I've worked with major clients including AGS Entertainment, Think Music, KVN Productions, Vetri E-Square, and many more.

How it works

From idea to artwork —
here's the journey.

01
You Reach Out

DM on Instagram or WhatsApp, or fill the contact form. Share your idea, references, and timeline. Free consultation.

02
Brief & Quote

We discuss scope, style, and pricing. A detailed quote with clear timelines is sent within 24 hours.

03
Sketch & Approve

Initial sketch shared for your feedback. Refined until composition, mood and style are perfect.

04
Final Delivery

High-res final artwork delivered in your preferred format (PNG, PDF, AI, PSD). Ready to print or publish.
